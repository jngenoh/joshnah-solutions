How we work

How a census project runs

Every project follows the same five stages, so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 1

    Plan

    We agree the scope, the data to collect, the forms and the timeline with your team.

  2. 2

    Train

    Our trainers prepare enumerators on the forms, the GPS devices and how to work with growers.

  3. 3

    Collect & map

    Enumerators visit every farm, fill the forms and walk the boundaries with Garmin GPS units.

  4. 4

    Clean & analyse

    We enter and check the data, generate polygons and produce grower statistics.

  5. 5

    Deliver & back up

    You receive the database, maps and report, with the data uploaded and backed up safely.
